# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Templates for the post list, posts, pages, tags, authors and errors.
- Light, dark and automatic colour schemes; accent colour, logo and fonts
  from Ghost's own settings.
- All editor cards, including wide and full-width images, tables and
  footnotes through the Markdown card.
- Members: Portal, subscribe form, members-only and paid posts, comments
  and search.
- English, Dutch, French, German and Spanish. The four translations have
  not yet been reviewed by a native speaker; if a label reads wrong, email
  support@aftertype.studio.
- No theme JavaScript, no font files, no build step.
